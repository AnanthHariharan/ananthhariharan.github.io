About

I'm Ananth. At the moment I'm a founding engineer at Blee, building the ML that sits behind legal and compliance workflows.

Before Blee I co-founded Bimini AI, a healthcare ML company. I built a HIPAA-compliant clinical platform and led a team on vision models for ICL surgery.

My research has wandered between NLP, computer vision, embodied AI, and computational linguistics. Outside of all that, I read a lot of Sanskrit literature and Indian philosophy, take photographs, and write essays when something feels worth saying.

Research

During undergrad I worked with five research groups across computational historical linguistics, conversational AI, medical imaging, computer vision, actuarial modelling, and HPC.

  1. Jul 2025 to Dec 2025

    CMU ChangeLing Group

    Research Assistant · Carnegie Mellon · w/ David Mortensen

    • Engineered a weakly-supervised framework for low-resource diachronic analysis, generating around 50,000 pseudo-labels from 100+ features to fine-tune mBERT with a dedicated confidence-estimation head.
    • Deployed a confidence-weighted ensemble fusing symbolic and neural predictions over a 1.47M-word Sanskrit corpus, reaching 87.3% F1 on gold validation with ECE = 0.043.

    → Publication (arXiv)

  2. Mar 2024 to Dec 2025

    Conversational AI Group

    Research Assistant · UIUC · w/ Gokhan Tur, Dilek Hakkani-Tür

    • Authored a ground-truth annotated dataset from the TEACh corpus and ran a systematic comparison of four SOTA LLM agents, setting a baseline of 100% precision and 90% recall.

    → Publication (arXiv)
