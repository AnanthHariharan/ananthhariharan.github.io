About

I'm Ananth — an ML engineer and researcher. Today I'm the founding AI engineer at Blee, where I lead the development of machine learning systems for legal and compliance workflows and run customer deployments alongside our ML R&D.

Before Blee I co-founded Bimini AI, a healthcare ML company, where I built HIPAA-compliant clinical infrastructure and led an intern team on proprietary vision models for ICL surgery — that work began as my internship there during undergrad and grew into a co-founding role.

On the research side I've worked across NLP, computer vision, embodied AI, and computational historical linguistics — most recently with the ChangeLing group at CMU and the Conversational AI group at UIUC. Two first-author publications are below.

    CMU ChangeLing Group

    Research Assistant · Carnegie Mellon · w/ David Mortensen

    • Engineered a weakly-supervised framework for low-resource diachronic analysis, generating ≈50,000 pseudo-labels from 100+ features to fine-tune mBERT with a dedicated confidence-estimation head.
