Wing I

The grammar of a building.

A building is an argument made in stone. The four lineages that follow — Classical, Medieval, Eclectic, and the Craft and Period Revivals — are four different theories of what a human being is and how that human should stand in the world.

The wing is laid out as four rooms. Each room covers one lineage, its sub-styles, the motifs you can actually point at, and the underlying philosophy. Walk them in order if you can — they answer each other.

  • Room I

    The Classical Lineage

    Renaissance · Baroque · Georgian · Neoclassical · Beaux-Arts

    Symmetry, mathematics, and the five orders. The long argument that the proportions of a beautiful building are the proportions of a beautiful human.

    Arts & Crafts · Tudor Revival · Indo-Saracenic

    Handcrafted honesty, cottage nostalgia, and the strange imperial hybrids that resulted when European builders worked with non-European traditions.
